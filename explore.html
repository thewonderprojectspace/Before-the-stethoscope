Thirteen paths into medical science

Explore the series.

Each series is a broad subject made up of focused sub-books. A sub-book is planned as a five-chapter learning path, moving from a clear foundation toward connections with people and practice. The titles below are a guide to the collection as it grows.

← Back to the homepage Visit the Digital Lab ↗
01

Physiology

Explore how cells, organs and systems work together to sustain life, adapt to changing demands and maintain internal balance.

CELLS · HOMEOSTASIS · SYSTEMS · INTEGRATION
02

Biochemistry

Follow the molecules, reactions and energy transfers that allow cells to build, communicate, grow and keep working.

MOLECULES · ENZYMES · ATP · METABOLISM
03

Anatomy

Learn the body’s structures, regions and spatial relationships, and see how physical form supports function.

ORIENTATION · STRUCTURE · REGIONS · RELATIONSHIPS
04

Histology

Look closely at cells and tissues to understand how microscopic organisation creates the working architecture of organs.

CELLS · TISSUES · MICROSCOPY · ORGAN STRUCTURE
05

Genetics

Explore how genetic information is stored, expressed and inherited—and how variation contributes to health and disease.

DNA · GENE EXPRESSION · INHERITANCE · VARIATION
06

Immunology

Understand how the immune system identifies challenges, coordinates defence, develops memory and sometimes harms the body it protects.

INNATE DEFENCE · ANTIBODIES · T CELLS · TOLERANCE
07

Microbiology

Meet the bacteria, viruses, fungi and parasites that shape human health, infection and the wider living world.

MICROBES · INFECTION · HOST RESPONSE · RESISTANCE
08

Pharmacology

Trace how medicines interact with the body, how their effects vary, and how benefit and harm are considered in care.

DRUG TARGETS · DOSE · PHARMACOKINETICS · SAFETY
09

Pathology

Study disease through the changes it produces in cells, tissues and organs, linking mechanisms to the experiences of illness.

CELL INJURY · INFLAMMATION · REPAIR · NEOPLASIA
10

Neuroscience

Explore the nervous system’s electrical and chemical communication, and its roles in sensation, movement, behaviour and change.

NEURONS · CIRCUITS · SENSATION · BRAIN AND BEHAVIOUR
11

Clinical Thinking

Practise moving from a person’s story to careful questions, useful evidence and decisions made with uncertainty in mind.

PROBLEM REPRESENTATION · EVIDENCE · UNCERTAINTY · DECISIONS
12

Communication & Care

Focus on the conversations and relationships that help people feel heard, understand their options and participate in care.

LISTENING · EXPLANATION · CONSENT · TRUST
13

Evidence & Public Health

Connect research, prevention and the conditions people live in to understand health across communities and populations.

RESEARCH · RISK · PREVENTION · HEALTH EQUITY
A common thread

One person. One question. Many connections.

The series are designed to help foundational ideas meet: a cell’s membrane can connect to physiology, pharmacology and disease; a person’s story can open questions about evidence, communication and care.
