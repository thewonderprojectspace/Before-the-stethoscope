A connected way to learn

How to use this book.

Each chapter begins with a person and a question about what their body is doing. Follow the mechanism step by step, from molecules and cells to the wider systems they influence. Use the diagrams, cases and questions to test your thinking. Then return to the person: what does the science help explain, and what still needs to be understood?

The science matters. The person at its centre matters more.
