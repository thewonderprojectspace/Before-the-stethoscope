Why We Exist

Humanity belongs at the heart of healthcare.

We believe education should do more than transfer knowledge. It should help us understand one another, question existing systems and create a world in which every human life is treated with dignity.

01

Health

Making scientific and healthcare education approachable, meaningful and grounded in evidence.

02

Wellbeing

Recognising the emotional, physical and social experiences of patients, students, carers and healthcare professionals.

03

The Value of Life

Encouraging kindness, dignity, equitable access to care and respect for every individual, regardless of their circumstances.

Our Philosophy
Before there is a doctor, there is a human. Before there is medicine, there is someone who needs care.

Science gives us the tools to understand life. Humanity reminds us why that understanding matters.

Where It All Begins

The smallest foundations of human life.

Physiology Edition — Book I: Cellular Systems and the Human Within.

Explore the cell membrane, organelles, intracellular membrane systems, blood and cell division through scientific explanations, clinical connections and human stories.

Designed for learners beginning their journey into medical science, this book encourages students to understand not only the mechanisms of the human body, but also the person living within it.

A Letter from the Founder

For the Human Within.

Before the Stethoscope began with a simple intention: to make a difference in healthcare education.

What began as a series of books exploring the human body has grown into a broader vision — one that brings education, healthcare and communities together.

I hope we can build a world where knowledge is not a privilege, kindness is not an exception, and the value of a human life is never forgotten.
Isha Tiwari

Founder & Author
