01 · Homeostasis

A thermostat for body temperature

Imagine the body’s temperature-control centre comparing the current temperature with a set point. Move the slider and consider how effectors help bring temperature back toward a workable range.

Near the set point: heat gain and heat loss are broadly balanced.

Simplified model: real temperature regulation is more complex and varies between people and circumstances.

02 · Membrane transport

Which way will water move?

A cell is placed in a solution with a higher solute concentration outside than inside. Choose the most likely net movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane.

Choose an answer to check your thinking.

Think about water movement and relative solute concentration; this is a simplified osmosis scenario.

03 · Feedback

Build a negative feedback loop

Arrange the idea in your mind: a change is detected, a response is coordinated, and effectors act to reduce the original change.

Stimulus → Sensor → Control centre → Effector → Response

Why is the response called negative feedback? Because it opposes the initial change, helping return a regulated variable toward its range.
