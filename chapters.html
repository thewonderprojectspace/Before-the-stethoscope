The physiology bookshelf

One series. Many books.
Five chapters at a time.

Each book in Before the Stethoscope will focus on a particular area of physiology. The first begins with cells. Future books can follow other questions in the body, each with its own clear chapter roadmap and the same human-centred way of learning.

A repeatable chapter rhythm

Every chapter has a place to begin, a question to follow and a person to return to.

Use this frame inside each chapter, whatever physiology topic the book explores. It gives the series a familiar rhythm without making every chapter feel the same.

The chapter template

For each chapter, plan these six parts. The story and science change with the topic; the learner’s route through them stays clear.

01 · Begin with a personOpen with a brief, respectful human story or situation.
02 · Ask the physiology questionName the puzzle the chapter will help the reader understand.
03 · Trace the mechanismExplain the process in a clear order, from first change to outcome.
04 · Connect the systemsShow the related signals, structures or body systems that shape it.
05 · Return to the personConsider what the science helps explain and what still needs listening.
06 · Check understandingUse a case, prediction, diagram or question to let the learner try the idea.
