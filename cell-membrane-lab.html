The foundation

Phospholipid bilayer

Each phospholipid has a water-attracting head and water-avoiding tails. In a watery environment, they arrange themselves into two layers, with the tails tucked inside.

Why this mattersThe bilayer creates a flexible boundary. Small non-polar molecules can pass through more easily than charged or strongly polar substances.
Look for the pattern: the membrane is selective, fluid and organised. Its parts work together; no single structure explains everything a cell can do.
