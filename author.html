A note from the author

From the Author.

A little about the intention behind these books and the way I hope they will be used.

Medicine asks us to learn an extraordinary amount: how cells work, how systems respond, how illness changes the body, and how evidence can guide decisions. It can be easy to let the volume of information become the whole purpose.

These books begin with a person because science becomes meaningful when it helps us understand something about a life. Each chapter follows a question through the underlying mechanisms, then returns to the person and asks what the science explains—and what still needs care, curiosity or further understanding.

Learning takes time. Some ideas will make sense quickly; others may need another explanation, a new diagram or a pause before they settle. Use the questions to practise your thinking, return to the chapters when you need them, and let understanding grow through steady effort.

I hope these books help you see the science clearly—and remember the person at its centre.

Thank you for reading and for bringing your own questions to the work.

With care,
Isha Tiwari
